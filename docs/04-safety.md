# Safety and material review

Planning record, not a laboratory approval. No exact material has been approved in this repository. RG1 labels alone do not establish eligibility or facility readiness.

The [official iGEM rules](https://competition.igem.org/about/rules-and-policies) require material and activity review and appropriate local oversight. The [official safety requirements](https://teams.igem.org/5986/safety/requirements) state that High School teams must stay within the White List and cannot use Check-In forms to work outside it. The [7 November follow-up rule review](11-igem-rule-evidence.md) accessed the current [White List](https://responsibility.igem.org/guidance/white-list): T7 is named, whole T3 is not, and other phages require Check-In. That route is unavailable to High School teams, including via outside collaborators. Keep T3 as a computational/literature reference while its proposed laboratory scope is unresolved. Isolated parts and intact genomes require separate classification. Apply the 2027 version when available; RG1 status alone does not establish eligibility.

Track each exact strain, phage, part source and activity in [strains](../wetlab/strains.md), including reviewer, rule version/date and source evidence. Confirm named supervision, facility access and local risk assessment before acquisition or work. The chat does not verify that the school, private laboratory or NUS is available or suitable.

Containment plan to finalize with the supervisor: access control, closed and labeled material storage, authorized transport, facility-approved decontamination/waste procedures and incident reporting. Clinical/environmental isolates and resistance engineering are outside the current scope. Sensor bench work is separate from any human use or diagnostic validation; no participant study is planned here.

## Awards and the evidence TipCraft should produce

The highest all-around target for a High School team is the **High School Grand Prize / Green BioBrick Trophy**. Gold is a criteria-based medal, while Special Awards recognize particular strengths. These are separate outcomes. [Official Project Awards](https://competition.igem.org/judging/project-prizes)

| Official source, checked 2026-11-07 | Verified rule or judging focus | Concrete contribution to prepare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Medals — current route](https://competition.igem.org/judging/awards/medals), 2026 | Bronze requires deliverables, accurate attributions and a useful contribution; Silver adds engineering iteration and Human Practices. Gold additionally requires three selected Special Awards, with at least one General Bio Engineering and one Specialization. Each must meet its performance standard; winning those awards is not required. | A reusable benchmark; a documented design–build–test–learn cycle that includes the WIsH failure; clear evidence of how relevant feedback changed project choices. |
| [Medal assessment](https://competition.igem.org/judging/awards/medals), 2026 | Criteria pass through judge yes/no outcomes, with ties favoring the team. From 2026, Gold votes are inferred from the three selected award rubrics, rather than a separate Gold vote. | Organize three substantial evidence streams; do not substitute a large list of proposed awards for completed work. |
| [Project Awards](https://competition.igem.org/judging/project-prizes), 2026 | Overall ranking uses weighted Project, Presentation and Wiki ballots. Questions include engineering, results supporting claims, impact, Human Practices, documentation and student leadership. Question-specific score descriptors are not public. | A coherent project argument supported by inspectable results and a student-led explanation of the methods, decisions and limitations. No private scoring formula can be reverse-engineered from the public scale. |
| [Best Software](https://competition.igem.org/judging/awards/special#best-software), 2026 | Eligibility requires documented software, an OSI-approved license and source in the dedicated iGEM GitLab repository. Software & AI Village teams are ineligible for this award. The rubric addresses validation, standards, utility, integration, usability and maintainability. | Reproducible installation and evaluation, useful evidence formats, clear interfaces and external-user feedback. GitHub remains a development repository; the required iGEM-hosted submission is a separate deliverable. |
| [Best Model / Best Measurement](https://competition.igem.org/judging/awards/special), 2026 | Model judging considers methodological quality, relevant validation data and influence on understanding/design. Measurement judging considers reproducibility, usefulness and appropriate validation/calibration, including computational approaches. | A frozen independent comparison and a decision trace showing what changed because of the result. Nominate Measurement only if a substantive measurement contribution is developed. |
| [Integrated Human Practices](https://competition.igem.org/judging/awards/special), 2026 | Relevant engagement should influence the project and support its responsibility and value. | Record stakeholder needs, the resulting engineering changes and follow-up evaluation. No outreach or endorsement is claimed from an unsent question list. |

The current three-award limit makes **Model + Software + Integrated Human Practices** a coherent provisional route if the final work supports it and the chosen village preserves Software eligibility. Measurement or Part Collection can replace a selection when their evidence is stronger. This is a project-planning recommendation, not a team nomination or an official scoring prediction. Village choice should describe the actual contribution; the title “platform” does not establish Foundational Advance suitability.

## Material and activity boundaries

| Official source, checked 2026-11-07 | Verified rule | Consequence for this project |

| [White List](https://responsibility.igem.org/guidance/white-list), current guidance | Listed bacteriophages include T2, T4, **T7**, M13, P1, ΦX174 and λ, subject to the stated virulence-factor exclusion. **T3 is not named**; other viruses/phages require Check-In. | Keep T3 as a published/computational reference. | [White List — activities](https://responsibility.igem.org/guidance/white-list) | Altering host range is listed in the context of making a **hazardous biological agent** more hazardous. | Do not generalize this wording into a categorical ban on every benign phage change. The exact proposed work still needs a documented material/activity classification before laboratory work. |
| [Safety policies](https://responsibility.igem.org/safety-policies) | GMO deployment outside the laboratory and human testing are restricted; human-subject research must follow relevant institutional and legal requirements. | The platform deliverable does not require environmental release, therapeutic testing or human exposure. Plan any software user study through the appropriate institutional process. |

The High School requirements page contains older season dates; only its stated section restriction is used here. The White List was readable in this review, updating the access limitation recorded in the earlier safety note. An isolated protein/part and an intact viral genome have different classifications; neither is automatically covered by the other's status.

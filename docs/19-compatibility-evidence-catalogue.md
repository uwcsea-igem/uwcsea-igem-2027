# Compatibility evidence catalogue

Updated 2026-11-07. **Eight named published observations now form a source-linked catalogue that separates binding, functional infection and direct assembly.** It preserves positive and negative contrasts without assigning an assembly label from plaque formation. The existing ten-case host benchmark remains unchanged.

The [machine-readable catalogue](../data/processed/compatibility-evidence.json) contains three publications and four contrast groups:

| Published comparison | Recorded endpoint | What it contributes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ando 2015: C-terminal versus full 13a fiber transfer on BW25113 | Two conditional infection outcomes | Donor identity alone does not establish transfer behavior. |
| Dunne 2019: recombinant GFP-gp15FL on wild-type and glycosylation-mutant hosts | Two direct binding observations | Binding depends on the tested host glycan context. |
| Dunne 2019: named stem chimera on the SV6b host | One conditional infection negative | Plaque outcome is distinct from spot clearing; the exact-row binding label remains unknown pending supplementary material mapping. |
| Ogari/Yehl 2025 online / 2026 issue: permissive complementation control, T7-fiber display and T3-fiber hybrid contexts | Three conditional infection observations | The permissive control prevents a failed target-host phenotype from being automatically called assembly failure. |

Each record has a construct name, host/complementation context, primary source locator, endpoint-specific outcomes, publication/contrast grouping and molecular-input status. Source URLs and interpretation limits are in the catalogue. Main text and captions were reviewed; no uninspected supplementary panel is presented as a direct measurement. The literature selected these examples retrospectively, so the rows are neither independent replicates nor a success-rate denominator.

**All eight direct assembly/incorporation outcomes remain unknown, and exact molecular-input bundles remain unauthenticated.** The catalogue therefore supports endpoint definition and source curation, with HOLD for assembly-classifier calibration. It does not show that direct assembly evidence is absent from the wider literature. Missing binding or assembly observations cannot be replaced by infection labels, structural confidence or a binder-design success rate.

Further work should authenticate exact materials and source figures, seek direct incorporation/oligomerization observations with matched functional context, and freeze each new case's role before scoring. Listeria observations are conceptual cross-system evidence and do not calibrate gp17. This catalogue generates no sequences, scores, construct instructions or molecular exports.

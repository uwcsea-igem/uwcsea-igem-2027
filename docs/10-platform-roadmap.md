# TipCraft platform roadmap

Long-term objective recorded 2026-11-07; implementation advances through evidence-based milestones. **Build a reusable recognition-interface standard and research software that helps others assess modular phage recognition within a demonstrated operating domain.** TipCraft is the software name. The biological project name remains a team decision.

## Intended contribution and source of the goal

The long-term product vision is a reusable tail-fiber scaffold, interchangeable recognition modules, an open characterization dataset and software connecting target information to candidate assessment. The requester reaffirmed this goal on 7 November. The WhatsApp discussion proposed the platform on 4 November at 15:08 and described the target-structure-to-candidate/DNA software vision on 5 November at 20:23. The later discussion favored Level 2, standalone protein binding, with computational work as a usable deliverable in its own right. Those messages were rechecked against the supplied private export; the export and unrelated personal content stay outside version control.

The original proposals of 12–16 characterized tips, approximately 25 strains and December/February decision gates are planning targets. Scope and quantities should follow the data, facilities and final team decisions. Current work is public-case retrospective evaluation; autonomous design and DNA export remain future software capabilities.

The November 7 receptor clarification separates native LPS controls from future protein targeting. The [Classroom and primary-source follow-up](21-receptor-dependence-and-source-check.md) records that intent, corrects the proposed shared knockout-control labels and keeps direct binding, genetic dependence, entry and infection distinct. The combined table and exact stocks remain unverified; this discussion does not change the previously stated scope preference or authorize biological work.

The [prior-art review](12-prior-art-and-innovation.md) establishes that modular phage swaps and protein-binder design already exist. The research opportunity is to define **when a recognition module remains useful after transfer**, and to encode that operating domain in a reusable, evidence-backed standard. A domain can be narrow and valuable; “universal socket” requires evidence spanning the proposed interfaces and contexts.

## Active work packages

1. **Select an independent predictor with accessible, auditable inputs.** The [alternative route](22-alternative-route.md) removes the declined Picard whole-host download from the active critical path. Audit available model assets and independent receptor resources for actual strain/mechanism relevance and target overlap. Preserve the GenoPHI source, label and representation findings as an optional method-specific route; its incomplete input cohort is not an admitted training set.
2. **Run the next method under its own frozen contract.** Retain [the GenoPHI pre-score contract](../software/tipcraft/benchmarks/retro-t7-t3/next-method-contract.json); an alternative method needs a separate complete specification. Save training membership and parameters before fitting, then hash the learned model/reference bundle before target inference. Report feature collisions, missing inputs, actual scores and matching-subset baselines, including host-only attribution where applicable. Independence requires target-related supervised exclusions. A head-comparison backend needs its own representation evidence.
3. **Build a compatibility evidence catalogue.** The [first eight source-linked observations](19-compatibility-evidence-catalogue.md) now record construct/context, source locator, molecular-input gaps and endpoint. Infection, binding and assembly observations stay distinct; no direct assembly labels have been established in this bounded catalogue. Authenticate further cases and lock their role/grouping before scoring; retain unfavorable and unmeasured cases.
4. **Test the software's usefulness.** Prepare a reproducibility task using only published inputs and compare the result with manual use of upstream outputs. Draft questions for future users about provenance, endpoint interpretation and abstention. Log feedback and resulting changes when engagement is actually performed.
5. **Prepare competition evidence continuously.** Use [the official-rule evidence matrix](11-igem-rule-evidence.md). Package a student-understood engineering story, reproducible outputs, sources, tests, contribution records and the required iGEM-hosted software submission when 2027 routes open.

## Excellence target

The all-around ambition is the High School Grand Prize. A coherent provisional award pathway is **Model + Software + Integrated Human Practices**, subject to the actual contribution and 2027 eligibility. These streams reinforce one another: a model changes an engineering choice, the software makes that decision reproducible, and relevant user input improves the tool. The official rubric rewards demonstrated work and student leadership; adopting the newest model or adding more proposed modules does not substitute for those outcomes.

Work prepared with AI assistance must be understood, checked and attributed by the team. This roadmap gives the ongoing project a direction; the evidence and decisions remain inspectable at each milestone.
